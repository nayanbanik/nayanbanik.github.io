- **Result:** Grand Prize, plus the Best Innovation and Creativity and Best Solution Efficiency awards
- **When:** February to April 2024
- **Host and sponsor:** UWM Connected Systems Institute, sponsored by Rockwell Automation
- **Team:** InnoHackers, with Tanvir Ahmed, Md Tanzil Shahria, Md Ishrak Islam Zarif, and Amir Foysal
- **My role:** System Architect and Simulation Expert

## What we built

We simulated and analysed transport options for the DEEP Cell testbed at CSI, where vial nests have to be moved between the loading robots and their storage space. Our solution uses a single OTTO autonomous mobile robot (AMR), modelled in Rockwell Automation's Emulate3D simulation software, with a return-on-investment analysis showing its advantages over human labour, conveyors, and automated guided vehicles (AGVs). The feasibility study modelled hardware limits such as speeds and charge cycles, and we presented the trade-offs and implementation paths to the automation teams.
