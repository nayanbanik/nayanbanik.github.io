- **Result:** Champion
- **When:** 20 September 2024
- **Host and sponsor:** UWM Connected Systems Institute, with Fortinet
- **Team:** Shadow IT, with Asif A Zubayer Swapnil
- **My role:** Team member in a two-person team

## What we did

A hands-on cybersecurity challenge built on the Fortinet Security Fabric. We worked through the anatomy of a kill chain to understand real-world cyber threats, then deployed advanced defence strategies against them.
