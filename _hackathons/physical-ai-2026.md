## What we built

Beyond Waste Sorting is an AI-powered robotic sorting cell that combines computer vision, motion planning, and an industrial robot arm. We developed and tested it in a digital twin, exploring Physical AI tools such as NVIDIA Cosmos and Grounding DINO in simulation, and then deployed it on a physical production line. The system verifies each pick and re-plans when bins move.
