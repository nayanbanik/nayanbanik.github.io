- **Result:** First Prize
- **When:** October to December 2023
- **Host and sponsor:** UWM Connected Systems Institute (CSI Hackathon 2023), with Microsoft
- **Team:** A.I.Chemist, with Tanvir Ahmed, Md Tanzil Shahria, and Anisha Tasnim
- **My role:** Lead AI Engineer

## What we built

An intelligent chatbot for the UWM CSI Advanced Manufacturing Testbed, powered by GPT-4 Turbo (1106-preview) through Microsoft OpenAI. It is designed for operational efficiency and real-time interaction on the testbed, and we benchmarked its performance against other GPT models. The assistant pairs natural-language queries with database lookups, and we ran user testing to improve its accuracy and response reliability.
