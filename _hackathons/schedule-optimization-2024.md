- **Result:** Grand Prize (92 points), after winning the Best Presentation Award in the first round
- **When:** October to December 2024
- **Host and sponsor:** UWM Connected Systems Institute, sponsored by Kimberly-Clark
- **Team:** OpTiS AI (Optimal Tissue Scheduler - Auto Implementation), with Gabriela Davila Albor, Saad Mirza, and Amir Khan
- **My role:** Optimization Algorithm Developer

## What we built

OpTiS AI is a heuristics-based, constraint-driven optimization engine for production scheduling. It analyses production demand and inventory data and generates an optimal dispatch list (planning schedule) that avoids overlaps on production lines, reduces downtime, maximises machine utilisation, and keeps inventory at an optimal level.

We estimated machine states and downtime from time-series manufacturing data, and defined the KPIs and test protocols for validating the model together with manufacturing engineers.
