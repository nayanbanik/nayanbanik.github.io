Professional Title PhD Candidate in Computer Science

Professional Summary

PhD candidate at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee and member of the BioRobotics Lab, working on assistive and mobile robotics. Recent work covers control of Mecanum-wheeled mobile robots and omnidirectional mobile manipulators, wheelchair-mounted robotic arms for activities of daily living, and studies with powered wheelchair users. Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering (on study leave) at Comilla University, Bangladesh.
