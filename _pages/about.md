Hi! I'm Nayan, a PhD candidate in [Engineering (Computer Science)](https://uwm.edu/engineering/departments/computer-science/) at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee (UWM), USA, and a member of the [BioRobotics Lab](https://sites.uwm.edu/biorobotics-lab/). My research is in assistive and mobile robotics. My doctoral research is on robust, constraint-aware control of 9-DOF mobile manipulators, and I also build AI-enabled robot control, including an LLM-powered voice and text interface on ROS 2. My goal is to contribute to assistive technologies and build smart rehabilitation solutions that help people with motor dysfunctions.

My recent work follows three threads: control of Mecanum-wheeled mobile robots and omnidirectional mobile manipulators, wheelchair-mounted robotic arms for activities of daily living, and studies with powered wheelchair users about autonomy, care, and assistive technology. I also work on partial evaluation in programming languages. The papers are on the [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page.

At UWM I am a Graduate Research Assistant in Mechanical Engineering and a Graduate Teaching Assistant in Computer Science. I am on study leave from Comilla University, Bangladesh, where I am an [Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering](http://cou.ac.bd/cse).

I received my B.Sc. (Engg.) and M.Sc. (Engg.) from the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering](http://cou.ac.bd/cse) at Comilla University and was awarded the Prime Minister Gold Medal. Before joining UWM in 2022, I taught as a Lecturer at Prime University, the Green University of Bangladesh, and Comilla University, where I was promoted to Assistant Professor in 2022.
